📘 About the Department

The Department of Electrical and Electronics Engineering (EEE) was established in 1994. The department has an annual intake of 60 students. It has been offering quality technical education in the field of Electrical and Electronics Engineering for many years.

Our curriculum is designed to impart both strong foundational theories and intensive practical skills in the laboratories. Students gain comprehensive hands-on operational experience with industry-standard machinery, instruments, and software systems.

🎯 Key Learning Outcomes

  • The department provides knowledge in Electrical Engineering, Electronics, and Electromagnetism.
  • Students learn about: Power Generation, Power Transmission and Distribution, Electrical Motors, Generators, Transformers.
  • The curriculum includes basic concepts of: Electronics, Control Systems, Automation.
  • The department helps students develop technical and practical skills required for industry.

💪 Key Strengths

  • Experienced faculty members.
  • Good career opportunities in the electrical and electronics field.
  • Industry-oriented curriculum and practical training.
  • Focus on practical knowledge and skill development.

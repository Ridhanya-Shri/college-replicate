📘 About the Department

The Department of Automobile Engineering was established in 1999. The department has been offering quality technical education in the field of Automobile Engineering and Electric Vehicle Technology for many years.

Our curriculum is designed to impart both strong foundational theories and intensive practical skills in the laboratories. Students gain comprehensive hands-on operational experience with industry-standard machinery, instruments, and software systems.

🎯 Key Learning Outcomes

  • The Department of Automobile Engineering was established in 1999.
  • The department has an annual intake of 60 students.
  • Offers quality technical education in the field of Automobile Engineering and Electric Vehicle Technology
  • Provides knowledge in Automotive Engineering and E-Vehicle Technology.
  • Helps students develop technical and practical skills required for the automobile industry
  • Curriculum includes basic concepts of Automotive, Emission and control, Fuels and combustion technology.

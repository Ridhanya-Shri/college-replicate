📘 About the Department

The Department of Computer Engineering, established in 1988, has been a pioneer in delivering quality technical education for over three decades. The department is committed to nurturing competent professionals with strong foundations in both software development and Computer Hardware technologies.

Our curriculum is designed to impart both strong foundational theories and intensive practical skills in the laboratories. Students gain comprehensive hands-on operational experience with industry-standard machinery, instruments, and software systems.

🎯 Key Learning Outcomes

  • Over the decades, the Department of Computer Engineering has consistently produced skilled diploma engineers who have achieved remarkable success in academics, higher education all over India, entrepreneurship, and the IT industry.
  • The department has a proud tradition of securing State Top Rank Holders and achieving Excellent pass percentages, with the majority of students successfully completing their diploma programme every year with high CGPA.
  • Comprehensive Learning: Specialized training in Software Engineering, Computer Hardware, Networking, Programming, and Emerging Technologies such as Artificial Intelligence, Full Stack Developer, and IoT.
  • Industry-Focused Education: Emphasis on practical skills, innovation, and employability.

💪 Key Strengths

  • Experienced and dedicated faculty members.
  • Industry-Relevant Training
  • Enhanced Employability Skills
  • Strong Technical Foundation
  • Value-Added Certification Courses
  • Industry Visits and Internships

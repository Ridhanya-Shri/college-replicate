📘 About the Department

The Department of Chemical Engineering was established in 1994 with the objective of providing quality education and training in the field of chemical engineering. Since its inception, the department has been committed to developing skilled engineers equipped with scientific knowledge, technical expertise, and problem-solving abilities to meet the needs of the process industries. The department offers a strong academic foundation supported by modern laboratory facilities and industry-focused learning.

Our curriculum is designed to impart both strong foundational theories and intensive practical skills in the laboratories. Students gain comprehensive hands-on operational experience with industry-standard machinery, instruments, and software systems.

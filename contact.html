HOW TO REACH US

🚌

BY BUS

Regular buses are available from Erode and Coimbatore to Perundurai. The campus is located 2 km from Perundurai.

🚆

BY TRAIN

Nearest station: Erode Junction (20 km). Taxis and local buses are frequently available.

✈️

BY AIR

Nearest airport: Coimbatore International Airport (70 km). Cab services connect directly to campus.

💡

Visitor Info: Visitors are requested to report at the main campus reception gate upon arrival. Virtual visits or video tours are also available on request.

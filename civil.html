📘 About the Department

The Department of Civil Engineering is committed to producing competent diploma engineers equipped with technical knowledge, practical skills, professional ethics, and lifelong learning abilities. The programme is designed in accordance with the Directorate of Technical Education (DOTE), Tamil Nadu, Regulation 2023, and focuses on planning, design, construction, maintenance, and management of infrastructure projects.

Our curriculum is designed to impart both strong foundational theories and intensive practical skills in the laboratories. Students gain comprehensive hands-on operational experience with industry-standard machinery, instruments, and software systems.

🎯 Key Learning Outcomes

  • The Department of Civil Engineering was established in 1983.
  • The department has an annual intake of 60 students.
  • It has been offering quality technical education in the field of Civil Engineering for the past four decades.
  • The curriculum provides comprehensive knowledge in structural engineering, surveying, construction materials, hydraulics, and more.
  • Students gain extensive practical exposure through laboratories, field surveys, industrial training, and internships.

💪 Key Strengths

  • Experienced and dedicated faculty members.
  • Well-equipped laboratories with modern experimental setups.
  • Industry-oriented curriculum and practical training.
  • Strong focus on hands-on learning and skill development.
  • Excellent career opportunities in public, private, and entrepreneurship sectors.

Our experienced faculty members are committed to guiding students in developing technical expertise, problem-solving abilities, leadership qualities, time management skills, project coordination capabilities, and professional ethics. I encourage every student to make the best use of the opportunities provided by the department and the institution and strive for excellence in both their academic and professional careers.
